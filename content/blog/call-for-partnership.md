**Partner with us for an exciting robotics competition in Accra!** Let's empower young innovators, promote STEM education and inspire the next generation of tech leaders.

![Call for partnership flyer for MRC Ghana 2027](/images/announcements/mrc-ghana-2027-call-for-partnership.jpg)

## Why partner with MRC Ghana 2027?

MRC Ghana 2027 brings students, coaches and schools together on **29–30 January 2027 in Accra** to design, build and program robots that compete in seven sports. Partners help make that possible, and connect with young people developing practical engineering, coding and teamwork skills.

Ways organisations can support the competition include:

- Competition venue and infrastructure
- Robotics equipment and arenas
- Student participation and inclusion
- Technical and volunteer capacity
- Awards and recognition
- Media and communications
- In-kind equipment and professional services

See all [partnership opportunity areas](/partners#opportunities).
