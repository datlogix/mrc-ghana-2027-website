**MRC Ghana 2027 will take place on Friday 19 and Saturday 20 February 2027 in Accra, Ghana.** The venue will be announced once it is confirmed.

> **Update, 17 October 2026: new dates.** MRC Ghana 2027 has moved to **19–20 February 2027**. If you saw earlier dates or flyers, please use the new dates.

## What is MRC Ghana 2027?

MRC Ghana 2027 is Ghana's edition of the Minoan RobotSports Competition, where robotics is played as a sport. Students design, build and program robots and drones that race, shoot, fly precision missions and play football, all under clear rules and against the clock.

The competition is organised in Ghana by **The MakersPlace**. The Minoan RobotSports Competition is an international programme of the **Hellenic Educational Robotics Organization (H.E.R.O.)**.

## Seven sports

The MRC Ghana 2027 programme includes seven sports:

- [Drones](/categories/drones)
- [Drone Soccer 3×3](/categories/drone-soccer)
- [Marathon](/categories/marathon)
- [Football 3×3](/categories/football)
- [Shooting](/categories/shooting)
- [Archery](/categories/archery)
- [Exhibition](/categories/exhibition)

Each sport has its own page with a plain-English summary, a preparation checklist, a downloadable information sheet and a link to the official H.E.R.O. rules.

## How to take part

1. Choose a sport and check the published age bands.
2. Read the official rules and form your team.
3. [Register your team](/register). Registration is an expression of interest: The MakersPlace reviews every entry against the current H.E.R.O. rules and contacts the applicant.
