The Minoan RobotSports Competition is an international movement that meets each year at the **MRC Global Olympiad** in Greece, organised by the Hellenic Educational Robotics Organization (H.E.R.O.).

## Global Olympiad 2027 at a glance

- **Dates:** 23–26 April 2027
- **Location:** Chania, Crete, Greece
- **Venue:** Chania Kladissos Indoor Hall
- **Edition:** 6th MRC Global Olympiad

Source: [he-ro.gr](https://www.he-ro.gr/minoan-robotsports-competition), checked 14 October 2026.

## Two separate events

**MRC Ghana 2027** (29–30 January 2027, Accra) and the **MRC Global Olympiad 2027** (23–26 April 2027, Chania) are separate events with their own organisers and arrangements.

## Is there a pathway from Ghana to Greece?

Any progression from Ghana to the Global Olympiad is **conditional**. It depends on the official eligibility rules, the national selection arrangements, compliance with the H.E.R.O. rules and any international places confirmed for Ghana.

Winning a category at MRC Ghana 2027 **does not by itself guarantee selection**. Selection also does not include flights, accommodation, international registration fees or sponsorship unless this is separately confirmed in writing.

Details of the national selection process will be published on this website. Read more on the [Global Olympiad pathway](/global-finals) page.
