Not sure where to start? Here is a short guide to the seven sports at MRC Ghana 2027. Each summary links to the full category page. **The current official H.E.R.O. rules always take precedence.**

## If you love flying

- **[Drones](/categories/drones):** a pilot flies a small, guarded indoor drone by remote control through a secret course of hoops, obstacles and landing pads. Precision and order earn points.
- **[Drone Soccer 3×3](/categories/drone-soccer):** alliances of three caged "drone balls" play aerial football in a netted arena. Only the Golden Ball Drone can score.

Drones and Drone Soccer are **different sports** with different rules and equipment.

## If you love coding autonomous robots

- **[Marathon](/categories/marathon):** a fully autonomous robot follows a black line around a track it has never seen. The fastest clean run wins. There are Basic (kit-based) and Advanced divisions.
- **[Archery](/categories/archery):** an autonomous robot bow adjusts its own aim and fires a blunt-tipped arrow at a ten-ring target from four distances.

## If you love building mechanisms

- **[Shooting](/categories/shooting):** teams build a handheld, sensor-triggered foam-dart launcher and aim at three scoring targets from two distances.
- **[Football 3×3](/categories/football):** alliances of three remote-controlled robots play five-minute football matches, using motor-driven kicking mechanisms.

## If you have an original idea

- **[Exhibition](/categories/exhibition):** the innovation showcase category. Detailed H.E.R.O. rules for Exhibition are awaiting publication, so follow the category page for updates.

## Team basics

For the six sports with published rules, teams have **2 to 3 members** aged at least 10, plus a coach aged 20 or over. Football 3×3 and Drone Soccer 3×3 are played in **alliances of three teams** from the same school or institution. Eligibility is based on age, not school class.

Every sport has a downloadable information sheet on the [Resources](/resources) page.
