## Flags for organiser review (inconsistencies and gaps)

1. **Age calculation is not defined** on any rules page (event-day age vs year of birth), and the bands share boundary ages ("10 to 12" and "12+"). Shown publicly as "awaiting clarification". *Decision needed: Ghana organising committee / H.E.R.O.*
2. **Exhibition has no published rules.** The page (redirects to /eureka-exhibition) displayed only its title, both via direct fetch and in a full browser. Eligibility, team size, project and judging criteria are unknown.
3. **Drone Soccer:** "only the specified Drone Ball model" is required, but the model is never named.
4. **Drones:** a 3-minute "demonstration" plus a "precision mission" are both mentioned; "DIY / 3D-printed micro" drones are excluded but DIY drones are allowed for Adults; the altitude-hold inspection may not suit every listed model.
5. **Marathon:** full autonomy and no wireless links, yet a remote start is mentioned for Advanced. A 3D-print exception refers to "Wrestling 1kg" (copy-paste).
6. **Shooting & Archery:** "Adults — only for the Advanced category" although each sport has one division. Shooting is "semi-autonomous" but also refers to autonomous operation. The Archery start procedure is described two ways.
7. **Football 3×3 / Drone Soccer 3×3:** alliances must be three teams from the **same school or institution** ("For 2027" on Drone Soccer). Ghana schools need three teams per alliance. Confirm with H.E.R.O. whether mixed alliances are allowed for national events.
8. **S1** still carries an organiser block headed "MRC GLOBAL OLYMPIAD 2026". Its early (to 25/2/2027) and late (from 16/02/2027) registration windows overlap. Olympiad fees are **not** published on this site.
9. **No national qualification rules or Ghana quota** are published by H.E.R.O. The site states that progression is conditional and not guaranteed.
10. No rules page carries a version number or last-updated date. The site records our check date instead.
11. The previous mrcghana.com stated **Heraklion** as the Olympiad city, a **"National RobotSports Arena Complex"** venue, and **"3 divisions"**. None of these are supported by S1–S9, and none are reproduced.
